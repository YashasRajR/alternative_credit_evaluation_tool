# How the score, the decision and the loan limit work

## The score (0 to 100)

Ten factors add up to 100 points. Each factor shows its points, its maximum and a sentence explaining why, so the loan officer can read the score back to the farmer.

| Factor | Max | Full points for |
|---|---|---|
| Past repayment record | 20 | Repaid every past loan on time. No history is neutral, not a penalty. |
| Existing debt burden | 12 | Repayments already due take a small share of monthly income |
| Land and tenure | 12 | Owned land, or leased land with a written agreement |
| Irrigation | 10 | Fully irrigated land |
| Harvest stability | 10 | No crop failures in the last 3 years |
| Other income sources | 10 | Livestock, non-farm income, more than one earner |
| Digital payment activity | 8 | Regular UPI payments |
| Savings and SHG membership | 8 | Saves regularly and is in a Self-Help Group |
| Crop insurance (PMFBY) | 6 | Crop is insured |
| Soil Health Card | 4 | Has a card |

| Band | Score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A | 80 and above | Very low risk |
| B | 65 to 79 | Low risk |
| C | 50 to 64 | Moderate risk |
| D | 35 to 49 | High risk |
| E | below 35 | Very high risk |

## The decision

The band and the risk model's chance of default are checked together. The stricter of the two wins.

| Decision | When |
|---|---|
| Decline | Band E, or chance of default 35% or more |
| Field review | Band D, or chance of default 20% or more |
| Approve with conditions | Band C, or chance of default 10% or more |
| Approve | Everything else |

A farmer who would be approved but has no room left to repay a new loan is sent to field review instead, with the reason shown.

## The loan limit

1. **Monthly income** = net income from the crop for the year, spread over 12 months, plus other monthly income.
2. **Room for a new instalment** = 40% of monthly income, minus the repayments already due.
3. **Repayment capacity** = that room times the repayment period in months.
4. **Credit need** = cultivation cost for the year plus 30% for post-harvest costs and upkeep.
5. **Limit** = the smaller of capacity and need, times a band factor (A 100%, B 90%, C 75%, D 50%, E 0), rounded to the nearest ₹1,000.

### Worked example: Savitri Patil

3.5 acres of soybean, two crops a year, ₹4,500 other income, ₹1,800 already repaid each month, 12-month loan, band B.

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Net from soybean this season | ₹29,972 |
| Monthly income: ₹29,972 × 2 ÷ 12 + ₹4,500 | ₹9,495 |
| Room: 40% of ₹9,495 − ₹1,800 | ₹1,998 |
| Capacity: ₹1,998 × 12 | ₹23,976 |
| Need: ₹59,929 × 2 × 1.3 | ₹1,55,815 |
| Limit: ₹23,976 × 90%, rounded | **₹22,000** |

She asked for ₹60,000. The tool suggests ₹22,000 because that is what her income can repay in 12 months.
